# User Personas

## 1. The Exam Candidate (Primary)
**Name**: Alex
**Age**: 25-30
**Background**: Junior IT support or recent grad.
**Goal**: Pass the AZ-900 to land a cloud support role.
**Pain Points**: Overwhelmed by the amount of content. Doesn't know what they don't know. Current practice tests feel memorized after 2 runs.
**Needs**: Clear structure, confidence indicators, explanations that make sense.

## 2. The Career Switcher
**Name**: Sarah
**Age**: 35-45
**Background**: Non-technical background (e.g., teaching, operations).
**Goal**: Pivot into tech via Azure certifications.
**Pain Points**: Tech jargon is confusing. Needs foundational concepts explained simply.
**Needs**: Adaptive learning that slows down when she struggles. Contextual explanations ("Explain this like I'm 5").

## 3. The Enterprise Learner
**Name**: David
**Age**: 30-40
**Background**: Mid-level systems administrator.
**Goal**: Employer requires AZ-104 certification for a promotion.
**Pain Points**: Time-poor. Only has 30 minutes a day to study. Hates reading long docs.
**Needs**: High efficiency. Needs the system to identify exactly what he is weak at and only test him on that. Wants hard, realistic scenario questions.
