# Company Values & Engineering Principles

## Core Values

1. **Truth over Assumption**
   - We measure, we test, and we validate. We do not guess what users want or what the code does. Data wins arguments.
2. **Quality over Speed**
   - We would rather ship one excellent feature than three mediocre ones. Quality is non-negotiable.
3. **Simplicity over Cleverness**
   - Complex code is a liability. We strive for elegant, straightforward solutions that are easy to understand and maintain.
4. **Trust over Marketing**
   - We build products that deliver on their promises. Our reputation is built on reliability, security, and user success.
5. **Continuous Improvement**
   - We are always learning. Every incident is an opportunity to improve. Every PR is a chance to make the codebase better.

## Engineering Principles

1. **We own business logic**
   - Infrastructure, databases, and AI providers can change. Our business rules and domain models are the core of our platform and must remain independent.
2. **Data is an asset**
   - We protect user data, we structure it carefully, and we use it responsibly to improve the learning experience.
3. **Documentation is code**
   - Documentation is not an afterthought. It is a critical component of the system that must be versioned, reviewed, and shipped alongside the code.
4. **AI assists engineers**
   - AI is a powerful assistant, but the engineer is the author, reviewer, and owner of the code. We do not blindly trust AI output.
5. **Architecture before implementation**
   - We think before we type. We use ADRs and design documents to align on the approach before writing the first line of code.
