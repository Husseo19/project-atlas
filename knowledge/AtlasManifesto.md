# Project Atlas Manifesto

**We build software that people trust.**  
In an era of generative AI, trust is our most valuable asset. We do not guess; we measure, validate, and verify.

**We optimize for learning.**  
Every feature, interface, and interaction is designed to make the platform smarter and the user more capable. The goal is mastery, not just passing.

**We believe engineering excellence compounds.**  
Shortcuts today are taxes tomorrow. We take the time to build strong foundations so we can move exponentially faster in the future.

**We value maintainability over cleverness.**  
We write code for the next engineer who will read it in six months. Simple, clear, and explicit wins every time.

**We own our business logic.**  
AI is a powerful tool, but it is not our core product. Our domain expertise and deterministic learning engine are the heart of Atlas. We rent the AI; we own the platform.

**We measure before optimizing.**  
Truth over assumption. We rely on data, analytics, and user feedback to drive our decisions, not intuition.

*Project Atlas is more than an exam prep tool. It is the future of deterministic, AI-assisted learning.*
